## My Favorite Series/Movie : **The Good The Bad and The Ugly**

---

### **About**
**The Good The Bad and The Ugly** is a 1966 Italian Western about three outlaws, Blondie (the "Good"), Angel Eyes (the "Bad"), and Tuco (the "Ugly"), who are in a race to find a buried stash of Confederate gold during the American Civil War.

---

### **Why I love it** 
I love *The Good, the Bad and the Ugly* because it really shows the characteristics of a main character, a villain, and a side character. Each one has a unique role that completes the story and adds depth to the film’s world. I also love it because of how mysterious the main character is, he doesn’t talk much, yet he leaves a strong and lasting impact throughout the movie. His quiet presence makes every scene he’s in feel powerful and meaningful.
